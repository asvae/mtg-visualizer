# Antiquities (`atq`) — standoffs

Genuinely uncertain calls, logged for later discussion rather than decided
unilaterally. Each entry: card name, what's uncertain, what was tagged in the
meantime (a best guess, never left untagged/blocking).

## Bronze Tablet

If its opponent declines to pay 10 life, Bronze Tablet permanently exchanges
ownership of itself and the targeted permanent. Commandeer covers changes of
controller, not ownership, so that clause has been left untagged. Ante and
Exile capture the card's other clean relationships. A future ownership or
exchange theme may be appropriate if the pattern recurs.

## Cursed Rack

> The chosen player's maximum hand size is four.

This restricts hand size but does not directly make the opponent discard.
Tagged `atypical` on Discard as the closest current theme; a broader hand-size
theme may eventually cover this, The Rack, Ivory Tower, and later cards.

## Goblin Artisans

Its activated ability flips a coin to determine whether its controller draws
a card or counters their own artifact spell. Draw and Artifact consumption
capture those outcomes, but the defining coin-flip mechanic has no curated
theme and remains untagged for now.

## Mishra's Factory

> This land becomes a 2/2 Assembly-Worker artifact creature until end of turn.

This is the first man-land encountered in the chronological sweep. Tagged as
temporary Creature, Artifact, and Assembly-Worker production, but the broader
land-animation pattern has no dedicated theme. It likely deserves a global
man-land-style theme once later recurring examples are processed.

## The Rack

Its damage scales with how far the chosen opponent's hand is below three
cards, but it never makes that player discard. Tagged cleanly as Face Damage
and `atypical` on Discard as the closest current theme. Like Cursed Rack, it
may eventually fit a broader hand-size theme.

## Titania's Song

> Each noncreature artifact loses all abilities and becomes an artifact
> creature with power and toughness each equal to its mana value.

The result is tagged as a Creature grant and Artifact consumption. No current
theme covers changing a nonland permanent's card type, so it is also tagged
`atypical` on Land Type Change—the closest existing type-change theme—pending
a possible general permanent-type-change theme.
