# Arabian Nights (`arn`) — standoffs

Genuinely uncertain calls, logged for later discussion rather than decided
unilaterally. Each entry: card name, what's uncertain, what was tagged in the
meantime (a best guess, never left untagged/blocking).

## City in a Bottle

> Players can't cast spells or play lands with a name originally printed in
> the Arabian Nights expansion.

This is an absolute set/name-based prohibition rather than making spells or
abilities cost more. Tagged `atypical` on Cost Increase as the closest
existing tax/prohibition theme; its sacrifice effect is tagged normally as
Sacrifice, Removal, and a partial Board Wipe.

## Eye for an Eye

> The chosen source still deals its damage to you, then Eye for an Eye deals
> that much damage to that source's controller.

This creates a new retaliatory damage event rather than moving the original
damage to a different recipient. Tagged Face Damage for the clean effect and
`atypical` Damage Redirection as the closest theme for the unusual reflection
shape.

## Guardian Beast

Prevents other players from gaining control of your noncreature artifacts
while the Beast is untapped. This clearly relates to control-changing but is
the inverse of producing a Commandeer event. Tagged `atypical` Commandeer,
alongside clean Artifact consumption, Tap consumption, and Indestructible
grant tags.

## Hurr Jackal

> Target creature can't be regenerated this turn.

This interacts directly with regeneration but neither produces nor grants
regeneration. Tagged `atypical` Regeneration; no dedicated anti-regeneration
theme seems warranted from this one effect.

## Pyramids

Its second mode replaces a land's next destruction this turn, but does not
grant the indestructible keyword and is not damage prevention. Tagged
`atypical` Indestructible as the closest existing protection theme. Its first
mode cleanly tags Removal and Aura consumption.

## Ring of Ma'rûf

The card puts a chosen card owned outside the game into hand. This is a real
selection effect, but the current Tutor definition only covers searching a
library or looking at its top cards. Tagged `atypical` Tutor, with its
self-exile tagged normally; a future recurring "wish" theme may be cleaner.

## Shahrazad

> Players play a Magic subgame, using their libraries as their decks.

The defining subgame mechanic has no existing theme and is unique enough not
to justify inventing one here. Tagged Life Loss for the separate clean clause
that makes each nonwinner lose half their life; the subgame itself remains
untagged.
